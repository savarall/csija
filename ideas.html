Blobs: spiky → smooth

White = ragged starburst, pink = softer lopsided star, brown = smooth pebble, green = lumpy flower. To do: tune sizes so all four look equally heavy, and make the spikes look less mechanical.

Unicode symbols

Radio = white, rain = pink, plane = brown, fern = green. Caveats: how they look depends on the device's fonts, and some may show as empty boxes. U+FE0E keeps the plane as a plain symbol, but most phones ignore it for 📻 and show the colour emoji.
