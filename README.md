# csija

*Csija* (pronounced "chee-yah") is what Hungarian parents traditionally whisper to a little one, much like "hush". This app is a soothing noise machine to help your baby (or you) drift off to sleep.

It's free, has no ads or tracking, and works offline.

It's also a vibe-coded app: built mostly by chatting with an AI assistant rather than writing every line by hand, so expect a few rough edges.

## How to use it

1. **Pick a sound.** Tap one of the four noises:
   - **White**: even, bright hiss, like a fan or radio static
   - **Pink**: softer and warmer, like steady rain
   - **Brown**: deep and rumbly, like a waterfall or the inside of an airplane
   - **Green**: calm, mid-range noise, like wind or distant waves
2. **Switch anytime.** Tap another sound and it gently crossfades. Tap the playing sound again to stop it.
3. **Set the volume** with the slider.
4. **Use the fadeout timer (optional).** Enter how many minutes you want (up to 240) and tap **Start Timer**. The sound slowly fades out over that time, so there's no sudden silence to wake anyone up. Tap **Cancel Timer** to keep it playing.

You can also pause and resume from your phone's lock screen or notification controls, so you don't need to unlock your phone in a dark room.

## Install it like an app

Open the page in your phone's browser and choose **Add to Home Screen** (Safari: Share menu; Chrome: ⋮ menu). It then opens full screen and works without an internet connection.

## Tips

- Keep the phone a little away from the crib and the volume moderate.
- Brown and pink noise are often the most soothing for sleep; try a few and see what your little one likes.

Sweet dreams. Csija, csicsíja.
